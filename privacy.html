Privacy Policy

This page explains what the PCM Notes iOS app and website store, what third-party services the website uses, and the choices available to you.

Effective 21 July 2026

1. The short version

  • You can use PCM Notes without creating an account.
  • The iOS app keeps reading progress and appearance preferences on your device.
  • The iOS app does not include advertising, reading analytics or cross-app tracking, and it does not sell personal information. RevenueCat provides aggregate analytics only for optional App Store purchases.
  • The Support tab offers four optional, repeatable tips through Apple’s StoreKit and RevenueCat. Tips unlock no content or features and are not subscriptions.
  • The website uses local browser storage plus Google measurement services and Cloudflare hosting.
  • If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

2. Information stored by the iOS app

The app bundles its chapter PDFs locally. It does not require an account or internet connection to read them. It stores the last-read page for each chapter, the selected System/Light/Dark appearance, and the iPad reading layout in Apple-provided local preferences on your device.

This local information is used only to restore your reading experience. It is not sent to PCM Notes. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to copies retained in your device backups and Apple’s restore behaviour.

If you choose to leave one of the four optional tips, Apple processes the purchase using StoreKit and RevenueCat validates and records it. Tips are repeatable, unlock no content or features, and do not create a subscription or paywall.

RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous App User ID for receipt validation, app functionality and aggregate purchase analytics. The data is not linked to your real-world identity by PCM Notes and is not used for cross-app tracking or advertising. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Apple may process purchase, transaction and payment information under Apple’s Media Services Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. Neither the developer nor RevenueCat receives your payment-card details.

3. Information used by the website

The website stores your theme choice and most recently opened subject/chapter in localStorage so navigation and appearance continue across pages. You can clear this using your browser’s site-data controls.

Like most websites, the hosting and measurement providers may receive technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, approximate region, referring page and pages visited. The site currently uses:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement to understand visits and measure site activity. Google may use cookies or similar technologies according to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare to host and deliver the site, protect it from abuse and provide operational logs/observability under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs to deliver fonts, interface icons and the PDF-generation library; those providers may receive ordinary network request information.

You may limit cookies or measurement using your browser settings, content blockers and Google’s available privacy controls. Some blocking may affect fonts, icons or PDF downloads, but the chapter images remain directly readable.

4. Messages you send

When you contact either support address, your email provider sends us your address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use that information to reply, investigate corrections and maintain the notes. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed for the conversation, recordkeeping, abuse prevention or legal obligations.

5. External links

Links to X, Ko-fi, TrustMRR, email providers and other external services open outside PCM Notes. Their own privacy policies and account settings apply. Ko-fi and TrustMRR appear on the website only; they are not integrated into the iOS app.

6. Sharing, sale and security

PCM Notes does not sell personal information. Information may be handled by the service providers described above, or disclosed when required to comply with law, protect the service, investigate abuse or protect people’s rights and safety.

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no device, email system or internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Students and children

PCM Notes is an educational resource and does not offer accounts or profile forms. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support message and want it reviewed or deleted, contact us using either address below.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may change when the app, website or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
