PCM Notes puts a complete handwritten Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics revision archive on your iPhone and iPad.

Pick a subject, search for a chapter, and open the full PDF immediately. Every page is bundled with the app, so reading, zooming, and returning to your saved page work without an internet connection.

INSIDE THE NOTEBOOK
- Physics: 23 chapters and 539 pages
- Chemistry: 37 chapters and 993 pages
- Mathematics: 31 chapters and 641 pages
- Total: 91 chapters and 2,173 handwritten pages

MADE FOR REVISION
- Search chapters by title or number
- Open every chapter directly in a full-screen reader
- Read in a smooth vertical, continuous layout
- Pinch to zoom and move quickly with the page slider
- Resume every chapter from your last-read page
- Share a complete chapter PDF from the reader
- Use a book-like two-page spread on iPad
- Choose System, Light, or Dark appearance
- Navigate through dedicated Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Support, and About tabs

FULLY OFFLINE
The roughly 500 MB notes archive is packaged as local PDFs. There are no loading links and no dependence on the website while reading. Install once and keep the full notebook with you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account or login
- No subscription or paywall
- No ads, reading analytics, cross-app tracking, or profiling
- Reading progress and preferences stay on your device

OPTIONAL TIPS
The Support tab offers four optional, repeatable tips processed by Apple through StoreKit and recorded by RevenueCat. Tips unlock no notes, content, or reader features.

EXAM COVERAGE
Physics and Chemistry are useful for JEE and NEET revision. Mathematics is organised for JEE Main and Advanced. Biology is not included.
