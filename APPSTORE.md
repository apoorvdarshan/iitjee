## Description

    PCM Notes puts a complete handwritten Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics revision archive on your iPhone and iPad.

    Pick a subject, search for a chapter, and open the full PDF immediately. Every page is bundled with the app, so reading, zooming, and returning to your saved page work without an internet connection.

    INSIDE THE NOTEBOOK
    - Physics: 23 chapters and 539 pages
    - Chemistry: 37 chapters and 993 pages
    - Mathematics: 31 chapters and 641 pages
    - Total: 91 chapters and 2,173 handwritten pages

    MADE FOR REVISION
    - Search chapters by title or number
    - Open every chapter directly in a full-screen reader
    - Read in a smooth vertical, continuous layout
    - Pinch to zoom and move quickly with the page slider
    - Resume every chapter from your last-read page
    - Share a complete chapter PDF from the reader
    - Use a book-like two-page spread on iPad
    - Choose System, Light, or Dark appearance
    - Navigate through dedicated Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Support, and About tabs

    FULLY OFFLINE
    The roughly 500 MB notes archive is packaged as local PDFs. There are no loading links and no dependence on the website while reading. Install once and keep the full notebook with you.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    - No account or login
    - No subscription or paywall
    - No advertising
    - No analytics, tracking, or profiling
    - Reading progress and preferences stay on your device

    OPTIONAL TIPS
    The Support tab offers four optional, repeatable tips processed by Apple through StoreKit. Tips unlock no notes, content, or reader features. PCM Notes does not use RevenueCat or another third-party purchase SDK.

    EXAM COVERAGE
    Physics and Chemistry are useful for JEE and NEET revision. Mathematics is organised for JEE Main and Advanced. Biology is not included.

    PCM Notes is an independent revision resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by IITs, NTA, JEE, or NEET. Always compare important information with the current official syllabus and trusted textbooks.

## Reviewer Notes

    PCM Notes is a native iPhone and iPad study reader with no account, login,
    paywall, subscription, advertising, or analytics. Every note and reader
    feature is available without payment.

    To review the main flow:
    1. Launch the app. Physics opens by default.
    2. Choose Physics, Chemistry, or Maths from the tab bar.
    3. Search for or select any chapter.
    4. The bundled chapter PDF opens directly in the full-screen reader.
    5. Use pinch-to-zoom, the page slider, previous/next controls, or Share.
    6. Close the reader and reopen the chapter to verify saved progress.

    On iPad, open the layout menu in the reader toolbar to switch between
    Continuous and Two-page spread. Appearance can be changed from About using
    System, Light, or Dark.

    The Support tab contains four optional, repeatable consumable tips. Purchases
    use Apple StoreKit directly; the app does not use RevenueCat. A tip unlocks no
    content or feature and does not begin a subscription. Reviewers may open
    Support to inspect the tip jar without purchasing.

    All 91 chapter PDFs are included in the app bundle. Core study content works
    without a network connection. The archive is roughly 500 MB, so a long PDF
    may take a moment to prepare on older devices.
